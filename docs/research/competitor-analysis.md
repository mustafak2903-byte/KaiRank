## Keyword evidence from the supplied export

The UK competitor export shows all four named source agencies appearing across the `healthcare seo agency` cluster. The most attractive confirmed commercial terms are `healthcare seo agency` (1,300/KD8) and `healthcare seo services` (1,000/KD6). The broader `healthcare seo` term has more volume (1,600) but higher difficulty (25) and mixed intent.

The commercial page should therefore lead with the buyer outcome and healthcare specialism, while titles, body copy, internal links and supporting articles establish relevance for the wider cluster. Repeating every keyword in headings would reduce clarity and is not recommended.

## Patterns to adopt

- State the audience and service on the first screen.
- Establish credible proof before extended methodology, but keep detailed numbers inside the case study where their context is visible.
- Use a direct conversation CTA.
- Keep service and local-search explanations human-readable.
- Support the commercial page with focused articles and case studies.

## Patterns to avoid

- Generic “full service” agency language.
- Long technical frameworks before buyer value.
- Unqualified traffic or revenue claims.
- Multiple competing lead magnets.
- Thin location or specialty pages created only because a keyword tool reports low difficulty.

## Current KaiRank differentiation

KaiRank now combines a clear private-clinic promise, a direct clinic visibility review, founder accountability, source-labelled proof and a genuinely optional website health check. The central distinction is not a larger service catalogue: it is senior strategic continuity, evidence boundaries and a calm premium experience that makes a complex purchase easier to understand.
