# Conversion refinement baseline

Reviewed 22 September 2026 against the public Vercel build and the `kairank-rebuild` codebase.

## What was already strong

- Distinctive black, navy, ivory and restrained periwinkle visual system.
- Editorial display typography, precise data labels and consistent evidence styling.
- Real case-study proof with periods, sources and limitations.
- Cal.com popup that lazy-loads after booking intent and restores focus when closed.
- Dedicated service, case-study and insight routes with canonical metadata and structured data.
- Public diagnostic with server-side input validation and no account access.

## Conversion friction found

1. The homepage asked visitors to understand the search system before it proved the result or made the next step clear.
2. “Check my clinic” made the diagnostic appear to be the main product. A clinic owner looking for an agency could reasonably mistake the site for a free audit tool.
3. Technical language such as “surface model”, “constraint layer”, “selection” and “entity clarity” appeared before buyer outcomes.
4. The strongest private-clinic proof sat too far below the opening statement, while the earlier corrective draft overcompensated by putting a dense metric dashboard too close to the hero.
5. Booking and diagnostic actions competed throughout the navigation and page.
6. Diagnostic results presented a pass count that could be read as a universal SEO score.
7. The public email referenced a domain that is not yet configured.

## Corrective direction

- Make the clinic visibility review the primary conversion everywhere.
- Keep the website health check optional and plainly labelled.
- Put a plain-language visibility-gap section after the hero, then introduce the Recovery Room as calm, source-aware proof after the service outcomes.
- Keep detailed Recovery Room numbers in the full case study rather than using unexplained figures as homepage decoration.
- Replace homepage technical systems with three buyer outcomes.
- Explain the engagement in three stages: understand, plan, improve.
- Keep technical detail inside service pages and expandable diagnostic detail.
- Preserve the existing visual identity, proof model, SEO route architecture and audit security.

## Baseline route inventory

Commercial routes retained: `/`, `/services`, `/healthcare-seo`, `/seo`, `/technical-seo`, `/local-seo`, `/ai-search-optimisation`, `/case-studies`, `/contact` and `/search-visibility-diagnostic`.

Informational routes retained: `/insights` and its article routes.

No redirect, noindex, domain migration or production deployment is part of this refinement branch.
