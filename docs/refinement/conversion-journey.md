# KaiRank conversion journey

## Previous journey

Hero → search-surface explanation → problem animation → technical capability system → outcome model → proof → founder → process → insights → diagnostic → FAQ → final CTA.

The journey demonstrated expertise, but it required too much interpretation before a clinic owner could answer three basic questions: Is this for me? Has it worked? What do I do next?

## Refined journey

1. **Hero:** healthcare SEO for private clinics, one commercial promise and a direct clinic visibility review.
2. **The visibility gap:** translate technical SEO problems into three patient-decision failures a clinic owner immediately recognises.
3. **How KaiRank helps:** treatment discovery, nearby patient visibility and a clearer route to enquiry.
4. **Calm proof:** show the Recovery Room challenge, work and evidence standard without dropping unexplained dashboard numbers into the homepage.
5. **What working together looks like:** diagnose, prioritise, implement and measure.
6. **Founder:** one accountable strategic lead, an authentic portrait and a public LinkedIn profile.
7. **FAQs:** answer commercial, operational, timescale and clinical-governance objections.
8. **Final conversion:** direct booking, followed by a clearly optional public website health check.

## Conversion rules

- Primary action: **Book a clinic visibility review**.
- Secondary action: **See real client results** or **Run the free website health check**, depending on context.
- The diagnostic never blocks booking.
- The diagnostic does not manufacture a universal score or imply rankings.
- The homepage establishes proof quality; the case study holds the detailed metrics, periods, sources and limitations.
- Case-study proof labels impressions, clicks, rankings and booking-intent actions separately.
- Kai provides three short routes rather than a second conversion funnel.
- No public email is shown until the business domain and mailbox are configured.
